## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one joke
about Monday mornings to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I tried to cancel my Monday-morning subscription, but it auto-renews every week and the cancel button is just a picture of Tuesday.

The line is 131 characters long. The joke lands on the judge's brief:
it is squarely about Monday morning, it is a real joke with a turn
rather than a complaint, it is specific and tight, and the twist is
packed into the last few words — the cancel button is just a picture
of Tuesday. No alarm, no coffee, no list of what is wrong with
Mondays — the un-cancellable subscription is the fresh angle.
Monday morning is named outright in the line itself, so the joke
cannot be mistaken for a line about any other day.
